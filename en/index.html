FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What guests usually ask before visiting.

Where is Trạm Nắng BBQ?

At 18 Đống Đa Street, Xuân Hương Ward, Đà Lạt, Vietnam. It is close to the city centre and has a large parking area.

What are the opening hours?

Open every day from 15:00 to 22:00, with no closing day. Last order is at 21:30.

How much does a meal cost?

Table-grill dishes start at 79,000 VND, small bites at 50,000 VND and hot pots at 250,000 VND. The BBQ combo for 2–3 people is 390,000 VND.

Do I need a reservation?

Booking ahead is recommended, especially on weekend evenings or for a railing table with the city view. Call or message Zalo 0865 751 768, or use Facebook Messenger.

Is there seating if it rains?

Yes. Besides the outdoor deck and lawn, there is an indoor area.

Can you host large groups?

Yes. The venue holds about 800 guests. Please message ahead for large groups.

Is birthday decoration free?

Yes. Table decoration for birthdays and anniversaries is free, in the restaurant's own style. The team can also help order a cake and flowers.

Is there live music?

Acoustic sets usually take place on Saturday evenings. The schedule changes by month, so message ahead to confirm.

How can I pay?

Cash or bank transfer.
